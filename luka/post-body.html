Luka Dončić has scored 361 points in his first nine games of the 2026-27 season, an average of 40.1, after putting up 53 against the Portland Trail Blazers on Nov. 6. Only one player has averaged more over a full season: Wilt Chamberlain, at 50.4 points in 1961-62 and 44.8 in 1962-63, according to Basketball-Reference. The Los Angeles Lakers are 8–2, first in the Western Conference.

Scoring more, on fewer misses

Dončić has made 110 of 221 shots (49.8%), 52 of 124 three-pointers (41.9%) and 89 of 102 free throws (87.3%). That works out to a true shooting percentage of 67.9%, a measure of points per shot that counts free throws and three-pointers. No player in the 30 highest-scoring seasons ever shot better. The closest was Joel Embiid at 65.5% in 2022-23, and Chamberlain's 50-point season came at 53.6%.

He is doing more than scoring, with 7.8 rebounds, 7.7 assists and 1.7 steals a game.

Six 40-point games in nine

Dončić opened with 28 and 23 points, then scored 52 at Utah and has not scored fewer than 37 since. He has six 40-point games and has averaged 44.3 points over his last seven. He missed the Lakers' Oct. 28 game against the Clippers, which they won 103-87.

Why it might not last

Nine games is a small sample, and hot starts usually cool. Dončić's last two full seasons, 33.9 points in 2023-24 and 33.5 in 2025-26, both rank among the 30 highest-scoring seasons ever. If he scored at last season's rate for the rest of a 70-game season, he would finish at about 34.3 points a game, which would still rank among the 20 highest-scoring seasons in history.
