One in 10 U.S. adults took a GLP‑1 drug in the past year

A CDC survey finds 9.8% of adults took Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro or a similar drug in the past year, including nearly half of those who have had diabetes. Four in 10 users don’t take it as prescribed.
