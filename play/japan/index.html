How Many Japanese Adults Say No One Is Very Close To Them?

The world's largest study of how people's lives are going asked more than 200,000 adults in 22 countries whether there is one special person they feel very close to. Guess Japan's answer, then see how it compares.

Method and data

Data: Global Flourishing Study, Wave 1 open data (Center for Open Science), collected April 2022 to December 2023 and weighted with the study's survey weights. The share is people who said there is no one special person they feel very close to and can confide in.

Your guess: stored anonymously (the game, the number and the time, nothing about you) so we can show how all readers guessed. The answer and chart come from the data above, not from your guess.
