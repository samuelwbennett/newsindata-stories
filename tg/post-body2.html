Ask Americans when they like to eat Thanksgiving dinner and they split almost evenly between early and late afternoon. In a 2024 Pew Research Center survey, 36% said they prefer to eat between noon and 3 p.m., and 38% between 3 and 6 p.m. Only 11% prefer the evening, and 1% the morning.

The NFL’s Thursday schedule covers nearly all of that window. The first game kicks off at 1 p.m. Eastern, the second at 4:30 and the third at 8:20. Because kickoffs are set in Eastern time, where you live decides which game lands on the dinner table.

In the Central time zone, a game is in progress for about 96% of the hours from noon to 6 p.m. On the East Coast the first game starts an hour into that window, so the figure is 79%. On the West Coast the Lions game kicks off at 10 a.m., and the Cowboys game runs from about 1:30 to 4:45 p.m., right through an early-afternoon dinner.

The Cowboys slot

The 4:30 p.m. Eastern game, hosted by Dallas, overlaps the 3-to-6 p.m. dinner window in every time zone in the continental U.S. It is also the holiday’s biggest audience. Last year’s Chiefs-Cowboys game drew 57.2 million viewers, the most for any regular-season NFL game, according to the league.

Not everyone is watching. In the same Pew survey, 35% of Americans said they were extremely or very likely to watch sports on Thanksgiving.
